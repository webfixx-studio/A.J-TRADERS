Wholesale FAQ

Are you a factory or a wholesaler?

We are a B2B wholesaler and trader based in Faridabad. We are not a factory. We purchase bubble wrap rolls in bulk directly from leading factories, maintain ready stock in our warehouse, and supply to businesses across India at genuine wholesale prices.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

As a wholesaler, our MOQ is very low. You can order from just 1 roll to 1000+ rolls. Factories usually require 50-100 rolls minimum, but we provide flexibility for small and medium businesses.

How is your price lower than factory price?

We buy in very large bulk quantities from multiple factories, which gives us factory-level pricing. We add a small wholesale margin and supply to you. You save on factory waiting time, high MOQ, and transportation costs.

What is your delivery time and process?

For ready stock items (0.5m, 1.0m, 1.2m), we dispatch same day from Faridabad. Delivery takes 1-3 days via courier for small orders and 2-5 days via transport for bulk orders PAN India. Custom sizes take 2-3 days as we arrange from factory.

Which bubble wrap sizes do you keep in ready stock?

We maintain ready stock of: 0.5 Meter Small Bubble, 1.0 Meter Standard Best Seller, 1.2 Meter Large Bubble, 1.5 Meter Jumbo Roll, BOPP Tapes, and Corrugated Cartons. GSM range from 40 to 120, length 100 meters per roll.

What is your bulk supply process?

Step 1: Send inquiry via Contact Page or WhatsApp. Step 2: Get wholesale rate within 30 minutes. Step 3: Confirm order with advance payment. Step 4: Same day dispatch with tracking details. Step 5: Delivery and GST invoice.

Do you provide GST invoice and quality warranty?

Yes, we provide proper GST invoice for all orders. We also provide quality assurance. If there is any manufacturing defect, we replace the material. We check GSM, width, and bubble quality before dispatch.
